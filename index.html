Internal Tools & Modules Revamp

As part of our operational evolution, we’ve developed and upgraded several internal tools and modules to support scalability, accountability, and performance visibility across all areas of Brand Management.

Enhanced Reviews & Ratings Module

Revamped the backend architecture and reporting logic to improve how we capture, categorize, and measure positive review performance across all platforms.

Complaints Module (Internal Tracking)

Launched a centralized tracking tool for both organically sourced and manually submitted complaints, giving us full visibility into recurring issues and response outcomes.

Review Incentive Program FAQ

Published a clear, internal-facing FAQ outlining rules, eligibility, and payout structure for the incentive program to ensure transparency and consistency across departments.

Negative Review Agenda Enhancements

Automated parts of the agenda process and clarified follow-up logic and ownership to reduce delays, eliminate missed escalations, and improve resolution speed.

Enhanced Reviews & Complaints Sheet

Redesigned our primary tracking sheet for both reviews and complaints, improving auditability, trend visibility, and real-time performance evaluation.

Expense Tracking Sheet

Created a centralized sheet to calculate all goodwill expenses tied to review removals and complaint resolutions; including elevated refunds, kick-ins, gift cards, and cancellations, providing better visibility and control over total spend.

Customer Success Module

Built to proactively identify customers who had positive experiences. This module enables follow-up, tracks post-claim sentiment, and supports targeted review collection strategies.

Customer Complaints Revamp

🔍 BBB Complaint Responses

BBB responses were standardized and structured with detailed resolution steps, increasing the likelihood of positive outcomes.

🧠 Review Analysis

Every negative review and complaint is now analyzed, with insights routed to Operations for training and process correction.

Success

Customer Success Revamp

🔍 Different Route

Customer Success agents now call every customer after their claims are completed, capturing fresh insights on their experience.

🧠 App Surveys

In-app surveys are now analyzed with deeper context, allowing us to uncover the “why” behind customer satisfaction and dissatisfaction.

🛠️ Review Verification

Review verification was tightened, creating a stricter standard that eliminates discrepancies in the incentive program and ensures only valid reviews qualify.

🤝 Review Posting

There is now a single point of accountability for review posting, minimizing errors and ensuring clean, audit-proof incentive tracking.

🛡️ Insights From Calls

All insights from calls and surveys are routed back to Operations, driving targeted improvements in process and training.

    We’re aiming for sustained improvement to unlock premium visibility on their site and ensure we’re featured in articles that AI tools frequently cite — these drive high-intent lead traffic.

  • Prepare for BBB re-evaluation

    Our current grade is a D-, but with recent complaint volume reductions and a cleaner record, we expect a significant upgrade in the next round of BBB evaluation.

  • Boost BBB reviews with a new incentive push

    A special incentive will be launched soon, specifically for BBB. Our goal is to maintain a steady 4.5★ average rating and increase volume of verified reviews.

  • Drive full-team participation in the incentive program

    With more frequent reminders and quick check-ins with TLs, we expect broader participation from all departments.

  • Launch customer referral incentive for GCS

    We’ll be rolling out a new Customer referral incentive program for the GCS team. This will drive lead generation while rewarding agents for proactive customer engagement.

  • Reduce complaint resolution time by 20%

    Once our Brand Management complaint handling module goes live, we’ll leverage automation and smart routing to cut resolution time by at least 20%.
